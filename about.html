About Invoiceo

Free, open-source billing software from Madcreations, a small software business in India.

By Madcreations · Last updated 3 November 2026

Who makes Invoiceo

Invoiceo is made by Madcreations, a small software business in India. We make free billing software for small shops and businesses, and we build custom features for businesses that ask for them.

The How it works page shows what the app does, step by step, and the comparison page shows how it differs from other free billing apps.

Where it comes from

Invoiceo is built on Invoiso, an open-source invoicing app by ANOOP P, released under the MIT License. Madcreations develops Invoiceo from that code and releases it under the same licence.

Version 1.0.0 was the first release under the Invoiceo name, in October 2026. The release notes list every change since, and the Licenses page has the full notice.

How it stays free

There is no paid version, no trial and no limit on invoices. Development is paid for by custom work that businesses ask us to build, and by donations through Buy me a coffee.

More in the FAQ: how can it be free?

Your data

Invoiceo keeps your business data on your own computer. There is no online account and no cloud copy. The only things the app sends are a daily update check to GitHub and anonymous usage counts, which you can turn off in the app.

The privacy policy explains this in full, and the terms of use cover the rest.

Invoiceo at a glance

  • Current version: 1.0.10, released 9 November 2026
  • Runs on: Windows 10 and 11, macOS 12 or later, and Linux
  • App languages: 8, including Tamil and Hindi
  • Licence: MIT, free to use, copy and change
  • Price: free; custom features are quoted per request

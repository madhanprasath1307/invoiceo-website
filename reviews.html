Custom feature

Sales and purchase on one bill, built for a farm-products shop in Tamil Nadu

The shop sells to its customers and also buys farm products from them, such as groundnuts or coconut. One bill now shows both. The final total shows what the customer pays, or what the shop pays the customer when it buys more than it sells.

What this bill does

  • Sale and Purchase items on the same bill: the shop sells to the customer and also buys from the same customer
  • Purchase items use the product’s buying price, and the rate can be changed on the bill
  • Grand Total = Sales − Purchase, shown as “Pay customer” when the shop owes the customer
  • Stock goes down on a sale and up on a purchase, and is corrected if a bill is edited or deleted
  • Receipt for 58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers, with a SALES and a PURCHASE section
  • No GST on the bill, and it works with the products already in the app

Custom feature

A tax invoice in their own layout, built for Sigma Healthtech, Thane

The company already had a bill their customers know. We rebuilt it inside Invoiceo, so each bill is made in a few clicks and prints in the same blue-lined layout, with IGST or CGST + SGST, amount in words, bank details and terms.

What this bill does

  • A tax invoice in the company’s own layout, copied from the bill they already used: logo and details on top, a customer box with the place of supply, and a blue-lined item table that fills the page
  • Product name with its description in brackets (only when there is one), HSN, quantity with unit, rate, taxable value and tax
  • IGST for another state or CGST + SGST within the state, chosen with one switch, and the two halves always add up to the paisa
  • Amount in words, bank details, terms and conditions and a signature box, with “ORIGINAL FOR RECIPIENT” at the top
  • It follows the app’s own settings: switch off the phone, address, bank details or any other field and it is left off the bill
  • The heading follows the title chosen on the bill (Tax Invoice, Cash Bill, Credit Note and more), and a long bill carries on to a second page with the headings repeated

See this and other custom work

About this custom work

Green & Green Agri Solutions, in Udumalai, works in agricultural products and solar power. Each month they bill for the wind and solar power they generate, and that bill has its own rules: units, a monthly rate and a list of deductions that an ordinary invoice does not have. We built the bill into Invoiceo for them as a paid customization.

The sales-and-purchase bill was made for a farm-products shop in Tamil Nadu, as a paid customization too: it is delivered as a private version of Invoiceo made for that shop, and it is not part of the free app.

The tax invoice layout was made for Sigma Healthtech, Thane, also as a paid customization: their own bill, rebuilt inside Invoiceo as a private version made for that company.

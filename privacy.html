Privacy policy

What the app and this website handle, in plain words.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

On this page

Questions? Write to us.

This policy explains what information Madcreations (“we”) handles when you use the Invoiceo app and this website. If you have a question about it, write to madhanprasat2002r@gmail.com.

01The Invoiceo app

  • Your business data stays on your computer. The customers, products, invoices, payments, settings and backups you create are stored on the computer where Invoiceo is installed. We do not receive a copy. The FAQ explains where your data is stored and how to back it up.
  • Anonymous usage counts. So that we know how many people use Invoiceo, the app (from version 1.0.4) sends us a short message when it is first opened, once a day while it is used, and once when the first invoice is created. Each message carries only a random ID made for this purpose (it stays the same on that computer, so one computer is counted once), the app version and the operating system (Windows, macOS or Linux). It never includes your customers, products, invoices, amounts or company details. The messages go to a small server we run on Cloudflare, which does not store IP addresses; Cloudflare’s handling is described in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. You can turn this off in the app under Settings → Software Info.
  • Update check. Once a day, when you are online, the app asks GitHub whether a newer version of Invoiceo exists; that is an ordinary web request, so GitHub sees your IP address. Apart from this and the usage counts, the app goes online only when you click a link that opens your web browser.
  • No online account. The app’s usernames and passwords exist only on your computer.
  • No advertising and no selling of data, ever.
  • Links you choose to open. Some buttons in the app open a web page in your browser (for example this website, our forms or Buy Me a Coffee). What happens on those pages is covered below or by that site’s own policy.
  • Backups and PDFs. Files you export or share are handled by you; we have no access to them.

02This website

  • Hosting. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Installers are downloaded from GitHub as well.
  • Fonts. The text font (Manrope), the heading font (Fraunces), and Noto Sans Tamil or Noto Sans Devanagari on the Tamil and Hindi pages, are hosted on this website. Your browser does not contact Google to load them.
  • Forms. Our support, customization and feedback forms are Google Forms. What you type into them (such as your name, email, phone number and message) is sent to our Google account so that we can reply. Google’s handling of it is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The customization page shows its form on the page, so opening that page loads content from Google, which may set cookies. If you would rather not, the other pages only link to the forms and load nothing from Google until you open one.
  • Analytics. This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and download buttons are used. It sets cookies in your browser for this. Google Analytics does not store IP addresses, and we have turned off its advertising features. We use it only to improve the site. You can block these cookies in your browser settings.
  • Email and WhatsApp. If you contact us, we keep the conversation so that we can help you and follow up.
  • The download form. Before a download starts, the website asks for your email or your phone / WhatsApp number (one is enough). The next section explains what happens to it.

03If you leave your email or phone when downloading

This is not a sign-up: no account and no password are created, and you do not need either to use Invoiceo. We ask only so that we can help you get started.

  • What we keep. What you type (your email, your phone or WhatsApp number, or both), and with it: which installer you chose, the page and its language, where on the page the button was, the country your connection comes from (as Cloudflare reports it), your browser and system in short (for example “Chrome on Windows”), and the date and time. We do not keep your IP address. To stop misuse, our server counts form submissions for one day under a code made from your IP address with a one-way hash that changes every day, and deletes that count early the next day (UTC).
  • Why. To help you install and set up Invoiceo, and to tell you about updates, by email or WhatsApp. We do not sell or share it with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Where. In a database in our own Cloudflare account (the same small server that receives the app’s anonymous usage counts). Cloudflare’s handling is described in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • How long. Until you ask us to remove it, or at most 2 years after you last filled in the form or our last message to you, whichever is later; after that it is deleted automatically.
  • Removing it. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 90255 37550, use the support form, or write to madhanprasat2002r@gmail.com, and we will delete it. If you only want us to stop sending messages, say so and we will.
  • In your browser. After you fill in the form, this website notes in your browser (local storage on this site) that you did, so it does not ask again on that browser. If our server cannot be reached, the download still starts, and what you typed waits in your browser until your next visit to the site, is sent once more and is then removed from your browser.

04How we use what you send us

Only to answer you, to provide support, to send setup help and updates if you left your email or phone when downloading, to prepare and deliver customization work you asked for, and to improve Invoiceo. We do not sell or rent your information.

05How long we keep it

We keep form replies and messages for as long as they are useful for supporting you, and delete them when you ask us to, unless we must keep a record by law (for example, invoices for paid work). What you leave in the download form is kept as described above: until you ask us to remove it, and at most 2 years after you last filled in the form or our last message to you.

06Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to madhanprasat2002r@gmail.com.

07Children

Invoiceo and this website are meant for businesses and are not aimed at children.

08Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. The terms of use cover the rest of how you may use Invoiceo and this website.

01Step 1

Set up your business

Do this once. Everything you enter here is printed on your bills, so the first invoice already looks like yours.

  1. Your name, address, phone, email, website and tax number (the box is labelled GSTIN for India and Tax/VAT No for other countries), in Settings → Company Info.

  2. Add your logo, and turn on the UPI QR code so it prints on every invoice. Bank details go in the same place.

  3. Choose the invoice prefix, how numbers are padded (00000007 or 7), the currency, the date and time format, and whether tax is on.

  4. Pick the invoice design and the page size you like. You can change them any time.

Good to know. A short first-run guide walks you through company details, invoice settings and the look. Several companies and logins can be added later in Settings.

02Step 2

Add customers and products

Add them before you bill, or as you go. Your lists grow with your business.

  1. Name, business name, phone, email, tax number and address. For a walk-in customer, just type a name while billing.

  2. Name, HSN/SAC code, selling and buying price, tax rate, stock, unit and expiry date.

  3. Already have a list? Import customers or products from a CSV file, and export them again any time.

  4. A USB barcode scanner works at the counter: scan, and the item is on the bill.

Good to know. Products that are running low or are out of stock are marked on the Products screen, so you can reorder in time.

Invoiceo

03Step 3

Create an invoice

Customer, items and totals on one screen. Tax is worked out as you type.

  1. Choose from your list, or type a walk-in customer’s name.

  2. Search by name (Ctrl+F) or scan a barcode. Add a one-off custom item with Ctrl+M.

  3. Change them on the line. Tax, extra charges and the total update at once.

  4. Make an invoice, a quotation or a receipt. Set the date, the due date and a note for the customer.

  5. Press Create (Ctrl+S). Not finished? Save a draft and carry on later.

Good to know. A quotation can be turned into an invoice, already filled in, and any bill can be duplicated for a repeat order.

Invoiceo

04Step 4

Print, save or share

The moment an invoice is created, you can look at it, save it or print it.

  1. The “created” card has one button for each. Preview the PDF first if you like.

  2. Several invoice designs, a thermal receipt layout, and your own theme colour and logo.

  3. A4, A5, A6, or a 58 mm or 80 mm thermal roll.

  4. Show your UPI QR code on the bill so the customer can pay straight away.

  5. Save the PDF and send it to your customer on WhatsApp or email.

Good to know. Tamil, Hindi and other Indian-language text prints correctly on thermal receipts, the same as on screen. More in the printing answers.

05Step 5

Track payments

Know what you have been paid and who still owes you, without a notebook.

  1. The Invoices list shows Paid, Unpaid and Partly paid, and the amount still outstanding.

  2. Full or part payment, by cash, UPI or bank. Each payment gets its own receipt number.

  3. The Customers screen shows what each customer still owes.

  4. Money collected, money still due, sales by month and your latest invoices, at a glance.

  5. Revenue, receivables, tax, customers, products, a daily report and stock. Export most of them to CSV or PDF.

Good to know. Reports use the buying price you saved on a product, so profit shows real figures.

A day in the shop

What a normal day looks like

The same five steps, repeated: bill, get paid, check, back up.

  1. Morning

    Open Invoiceo. Scan the customer’s items, create the bill and print a thermal receipt at the counter.

  2. Midday

    A customer asks for a price. Make a quotation, send the PDF, and turn it into an invoice when they say yes.

  3. Afternoon

    A customer pays half now. Record the part payment and the balance is kept for later.

  4. Evening

    Open the dashboard: today’s sales, money still due and items running low.

  5. Month end

    Run the tax and receivables reports, export them to CSV for your accountant, and make a backup.

Your data

Safe on your computer, easy to move

There is no cloud and no account. Your records live on your computer, and you decide when to copy them. Read the privacy policy.

  • Stored on your computerCustomers, products, invoices and payments never leave it.
  • Automatic backupTurn it on in Settings → Backup, and a copy is saved while the app is open.
  • Back up now, or exportMake a backup whenever you like, or export your data as JSON.
  • Move to a new computerInstall Invoiceo there, choose Import / restore and pick your backup.

Before you install

What you need

Very little. Questions? See the install answers in the FAQ.

A computer

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), macOS 12 or later, or Linux (a .deb package or an AppImage). Invoiceo is a desktop app; it is not for phones.

No internet, no account

You need the internet only to download it. Billing, printing and reports all work offline, and there is nothing to sign up for.

A moment on Windows

Windows may ask before it opens the installer, because it is not code-signed yet. The Download page shows the two clicks, with pictures.

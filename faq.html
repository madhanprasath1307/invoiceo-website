Short answers about Invoiceo. Can’t find yours? Ask us.

By Madcreations · Last updated 3 November 2026

About Invoiceo

Is Invoiceo really free?

Yes. The app and its updates are free, with no subscription and no trial period. You can install it on as many of your computers as you like.

We only charge for work done for you on request, such as a new bill format or a report. See Customization.

Is Invoiceo open source?

Yes. Invoiceo is released under the MIT License, so anyone can read, use and change the code. It is based on Invoiso by ANOOP P, which is also MIT-licensed. See about Invoiceo and the licences.

How can it be free?

There is no paid version. Development is supported by optional paid customization for businesses that need something special, and by donations.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Invoiceo runs on your computer, so you can bill, print and see reports offline. You need a connection only to download the installer, to open our website or forms, or to share a PDF online.

Do I need to create an account?

No online account is needed. The app has its own login on your computer, so you can protect it with a password and give each person their own username. Before your first download the website asks for an email or phone number so that we can help you set it up; that is not an account, and nothing is needed to use the app.

Where is my data stored?

Everything you enter is saved on your own computer, in the app’s data folder. Your customers, products and invoices are not sent to us.

Because the data is on your computer, keeping it safe is in your hands: use Settings → Backup in the app regularly.

Does Invoiceo collect any data?

Only a few anonymous usage counts, so we know how many people use the app: when it is first opened, once a day while it is used, and once when the first invoice is made. Each count carries a random ID (the same one on that computer), the app version and the operating system, nothing else.

Your customers, products, invoices, amounts and company details are never sent. You can turn the counts off in Settings → Software Info.

Separately, the email or phone number you leave on this website before a download is kept only to help you set up and to share updates, and is removed when you ask. See the privacy policy.

Billing and printing

Can I use Invoiceo for GST billing?

Yes. You can add your GSTIN and your customers’ GSTINs, HSN/SAC codes and tax rates per product or for the whole invoice. Invoices show CGST and SGST, or IGST for sales to another state, and you can choose titles such as Tax Invoice or Bill of Supply.

Invoiceo helps you prepare correct invoices; for filing returns, please follow your accountant’s advice.

Can I use it in my country?

Yes, in any country. Choose the currency you bill in and one of eight app languages, set your own tax rates, and turn the GST fields off in the invoice settings when you don’t need them.

Which printers can I use?

Any printer your computer can print to, for A4, A5 and A6 invoices. For receipts, Invoiceo supports 58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers and can print the receipt automatically after each sale (in the Modern layout).

Can I use it in Tamil?

Yes. The app can be used in English, Tamil, Hindi, Nepali, French, Spanish, Chinese and Tibetan (partly translated), and Tamil names print correctly on PDFs and thermal receipts.

Does it work with a barcode scanner?

Yes. A USB barcode scanner that types the code works on the New Invoice screen: scan a product to add it to the bill.

Is it right for a small shop?

Yes. Barcode scanning, thermal receipts, stock levels, batch and expiry dates, and daily sales reports are built in. See all the features.

Can I manage more than one business?

Yes. You can add several companies on one computer. Each company keeps its own customers, products, invoices and settings.

Can more than one person use it?

Yes, on the same computer. Each person can have their own login, as an admin or a normal user.

Installing and updating

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), macOS 12 Monterey or later, and Linux: a .deb for Ubuntu 22.04+, Debian 12+ and Linux Mint 21+, and an AppImage for other distributions. There is no phone app.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is it safe?

This message appears for installers that are not code-signed yet. If you downloaded Invoiceo from this website, click More info and then Run anyway. The Windows picture guide shows both screens, and you can check the file with its SHA-256 fingerprint.

macOS says it cannot check the app for malicious software.

The app is not notarised by Apple yet. Click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Invoiceo and click Open Anyway. On macOS 14 or older, Control-click Invoiceo in Applications and choose Open. You only need to do this the first time.

How do I update to a new version?

Download the new version from the Download page and install it over the old one. Your data is kept, but make a backup first to be safe. See what changed on the What’s new page.

How do I move to a new computer?

Make a backup of each company on the old computer, copy the backup file to the new one, install Invoiceo there and import the backup from Settings → Backup.

Limits and what is not built in

Is there any limit on invoices?

No. There is no limit on invoices, customers, products or computers, and no trial period. Every feature is included.

Does Invoiceo file GST returns, or make e-invoices and e-way bills?

Not built in. Invoiceo prints correct GST invoices and gives a Tax report by tax rate (taxable amount and tax) that you can save as CSV or PDF and give to your accountant. It does not make GSTR-1 or GSTR-3B files, e-invoices or e-way bills.

If you need these, they can be built as paid customization. Tell us the format you need.

Can I send an invoice on WhatsApp or email from the app?

Not directly. Save the invoice as a PDF (or print it) and attach it in WhatsApp or email. A one-click send button can be built as paid customization.

Can I record purchases and supplier bills?

The free app records sales and keeps a purchase price and stock count for each product. A separate supplier and purchase section is not part of the free app; it can be built as paid customization.

Can two computers use the same data?

Not live. Each computer keeps its own data, and there is no sync between computers or a cloud version. The simplest setup is one main billing computer where everyone logs in with their own user. To move data to another computer, use Settings → Backup. Sharing data between computers can be looked at as paid customization.

How is Invoiceo different from Vyapar, myBillBook or Tally?

Invoiceo is free with no limits, works fully offline, needs no account and is open source. The others have more features, such as GST returns, e-invoicing and phone apps, but their free plans are limited or are trials. See the side-by-side comparison.

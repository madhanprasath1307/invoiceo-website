Which is right for you?

Choose Invoiceo if…

  • You want billing that is free for good, with no invoice cap and no trial that ends
  • You want your data on your own computer, with no online account and no cloud
  • You print on A4 or 58/80 mm thermal printers, or need Tamil or Hindi on your bills
  • You want open-source software, or a small custom feature made for your business at a fair price

Choose another if…

  • You file GST returns from the software, or need e-invoice and e-way bill
  • You need a phone app, or several shops or computers working on the same data
  • You want WhatsApp or SMS reminders sent from the software
  • You need full accounting with ledgers and a balance sheet

Missing something? Invoiceo is free, and features such as the ones above can be built for your business as a paid service, quoted per request.
